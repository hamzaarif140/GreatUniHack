# Demo script: Python 101 in 2 minutes

The students are live AI, so they never say exactly the same thing twice. Lines in quotes are said
out loud. Pause after each one: the class replies when you stop talking.

## Before you start (not timed)

- [ ] `npm run check` passes, and `npm run server` and `npm run dev` are running.
- [ ] Wear headphones.
- [ ] Start the class **before** you go on: **New class**, enter your name, choose **Another topic…**,
  type **Python 101: lists and loops**, choose the mood **Typical**, then **Start class**. Wait until
  all five students are seated.

## The demo

### 0:00 Intro (15 seconds)

> "New PhD TAs often start teaching with no training, so they practise on real students. This is a
> class of AI students you can practise on instead. Each one quietly tracks how well they're
> following, and you get a debrief at the end."

### 0:15 Teach it badly (30 seconds)

> "So, Python lists are mutable sequences, append is amortised constant time, and loops use the
> iterator protocol. It's a piece of cake. Does that make sense?"

**Look for:** moods dropping to 😕 and hands going up. A lost student, often Priya, says a hesitant
"yeah, I think so". **Say:** "That's the most dangerous answer in teaching." Don't call on anyone.

### 0:45 Teach it well (40 seconds)

> "Let me try again. Imagine a shopping list: eggs, milk, bread. Append adds tea to the end. A for
> loop then goes through the list one item at a time."

**Look for:** moods rising to 🙂 or 😃. Then **click a raised hand**, or call a name:

> "Leo, go ahead."

**Say:** "They raise a hand when they're lost, and they ask about the bit that confused them."

### 1:25 Debrief (25 seconds)

Click the red **End class** button.

> "Here's the dip when I used jargon, and the climb with the example. These are the signals I
> missed, and every point links to the exact moment."

Point at the **Missed signals** list as you say it, then click one timestamp to show the jump to the
transcript.

### 1:50 Close (10 seconds)

> "TAs make their first mistakes here, privately, with feedback, instead of on real students."

## If something goes wrong

| What happens | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Nobody replies | Call a name: "Marcus, what do you think?" Naming always gets an answer. |
| No hands go up | Say one more jargon-heavy line. Check the mood isn't Chaotic. |
| The mic mishears you | Type the line in the box at the bottom instead. |
| Running long | Skip calling on a hand and go straight to **End class**. |
| Students stop responding | The Claude key may be out of credit. Run `npm run check`. |
